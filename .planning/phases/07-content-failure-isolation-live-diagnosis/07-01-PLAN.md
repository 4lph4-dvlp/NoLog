<objective>
Make the post-detail render's failure legible per leg, and stop a chrome-level failure from destroying an already-fetched post body.

Purpose: CONT-01 requires that an operator reading production logs can point at exactly one of `getPageRecordMap`, `getCategories`, or the related-posts `getPosts`. Today all three share one `try` and one identical `console.error`. CONT-04 is satisfied by the same change: under the current combined catch a categories failure nulls a `recordMap` that already succeeded, which is the precise mechanism blanking the post body. This plan also ships the on-demand failure-reproduction route the evidence-capture plan (07-03) depends on, because Vercel Hobby retains runtime logs for only ~1 hour and organic failure timing is hostage to the ISR cache.

Output: a gated deep-diagnostic helper in `lib/notion-x.ts`, a new secret-gated `/api/diagnose-page` route, and a two-`try` decomposition of `post/[id]/page.tsx` emitting `[PostPage:recordMap]` and `[PostPage:chrome]`.
</objective>
